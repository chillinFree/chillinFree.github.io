Research Goal

My long-term research goal is to develop robots with human-level versatility in perception, reasoning, and manipulation, ultimately enhancing everyday life at scale.

My current research focuses on dexterous manipulation, with an emphasis on leveraging human data to address the representation gap and data scarcity that limit today's multi-finger manipulation systems. I pursue this along two complementary tracks:

1
Real-to-sim & sim-to-real transfer

Reconstructing human demonstrations in simulation to generate scalable, physically grounded robot training data.

DexImit Do As I Do
2
Direct learning from human data

Using large-scale human hand–object interaction data to close the embodiment and representation gap for dexterous manipulation policies.

UniDex EgoScale
